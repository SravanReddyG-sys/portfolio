About

I'm a full-stack developer in training — the kind who's just as comfortable tracing a slow query in MySQL as wiring up an Angular form. My two flagship projects both share a pattern: take something surplus or disorganized (spare food, farm produce) and build the routing logic to get it to the right place.

Underneath the frameworks, I care about the fundamentals: how the OS schedules the process, how the network gets the packet there, how the schema should actually be shaped. That's the layer I keep coming back to.

Currently based in Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh, and looking for an internship where I can apply this end-to-end.
